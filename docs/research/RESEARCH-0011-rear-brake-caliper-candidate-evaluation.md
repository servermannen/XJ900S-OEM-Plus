# RESEARCH-0011: XJ900S rear-brake caliper candidate evaluation

**Purpose:** Define the evidence needed to evaluate rear-brake caliper candidates for the 1997 Yamaha XJ900S.

**Document status: Draft**

**Status: Unverified**

**Review: Technical Review Required**

## Research question

What evidence is required to determine whether any rear-brake caliper candidate
can be mechanically, hydraulically, serviceably and safely integrated with the
1997 Yamaha XJ900S rear brake system?

## Scope

### Included

- Candidate availability.
- Candidate identity.
- Donor application.
- Caliper-body geometry.
- Holder/carrier geometry.
- Piston area.
- Pad geometry.
- Disc geometry.
- Hydraulic compatibility.
- Hose/banjo compatibility.
- Wheel, swingarm, tire and hose clearance.
- Torque-reaction/load path.
- Parking or retention considerations if relevant.
- Service parts.
- Safety suitability.
- Final acceptance state.

### Excluded

- Selection of a rear caliper.
- Candidate ranking.
- Final component acceptance.
- Creation of component records.
- Creation of a rear-brake test plan.
- Modification of front-brake records.
- Road-use approval.

No part number, visual similarity, donor name, holder contact, pad similarity,
physical bolting, hose connection, firm pedal, free wheel rotation, or single
successful stop is treated as rear-brake compatibility evidence by this record.

## Original XJ holder/carrier evidence boundary

`1AE-25819-52` is treated here as a candidate/reference identifier only. This
record does not establish that it is the original XJ900S holder/carrier, nor
that any donor caliper can reuse it.

Do not state that a donor caliper can reuse the XJ holder/carrier because:

- Mounting bolt spacing appears similar.
- Pad shape is similar.
- The caliper physically sits on the holder.
- The caliper can be temporarily bolted in place.

Require direct verification of:

- Mounting centres.
- Mounting-lug thickness.
- Axial offset.
- Radial offset.
- Bolt size/thread.
- Torque-reaction/load path.
- Disc centering.
- Full pad sweep.
- Pad retention.
- Wheel/swingarm clearance.

Physical bolting-on is not final compatibility evidence.

## Analysis

The current repository evidence supports only a rear-brake research backlog and
an acquisition boundary for on-hand rear-brake parts. It does not support
interchangeability between the XJ900S rear caliper/holder and the candidate
identifiers `3GM-2580W-A0`, `5JD-2580W-01` or `5JJ-2580W-00`.

Mechanical compatibility remains blocked until the original baseline,
candidate caliper, candidate holder/carrier, disc, fasteners, torque-reaction
features, pad sweep and clearances are directly verified. Hydraulic
compatibility remains blocked until piston counts, piston diameters, total
hydraulic piston area, rear master-cylinder bore, pedal geometry, displacement
requirements, hose/banjo interfaces, fluid specification and pressure/thermal
suitability are supported by evidence and reviewed.

The on-hand items `AA5293`, `PKAG7185` and `SM-MVAA3733-BL-CA-61` are useful
for availability tracking only at this stage. They do not identify or validate
any candidate caliper, holder/carrier, hose routing, seal set, piston, pad
shape, friction material, installation method or road-ready rear-brake system.

## Conclusion

Rear-brake caliper candidate evaluation is open. The required evidence is
identity-first, measurement-first and review-gated. No rear caliper, holder,
pad set, piston/seal kit, hose, hydraulic configuration or rear-brake assembly
is selected or accepted by this record.
