## Required validation

- Verify Yamaha application and normalized OEM part number from an authoritative
  Yamaha parts source.
- Photograph all markings, clamp areas, bends, ends, locating holes, internal
  features, finish, corrosion, and damage before installation or modification.
- Measure diameter, clamp width, total width, rise, sweep, pullback, straight
  control lengths, bar-end configuration, and any locating features using a
  documented method.
- Compare the candidate with the original XJ900S handlebar and the requirements
  of the intended controls without inferring dimensions from photographs.
- Inspect straightness, cracking, deformation, corrosion, prior drilling,
  grinding, welding, or other modification.
- Verify control retention and complete cable, hose, and wiring routing through
  the full steering range.
- Verify clearance to the tank, fairing, screen, instruments, levers, reservoirs,
  and rider before any road use.
- Define acceptance criteria and complete technical review before installation
  is accepted.
