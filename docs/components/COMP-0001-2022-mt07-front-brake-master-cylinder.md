## Decision recommendation

**Recommendation: Bench evaluate**

**Rationale:** The acquired assembly can be inspected and measured, but its
identity, condition, specifications, hydraulic relationship to the intended
calipers, physical integration, and safety remain unverified. Acquisition does
not constitute component acceptance.

Do not use `Accept` while material compatibility questions remain unresolved.

## Required validation

- Verify the exact Yamaha OEM part number and 2022 MT-07 application using an
  official Yamaha parts catalogue or other authoritative Yamaha documentation.
- Establish the master-cylinder piston diameter through authoritative
  documentation or documented direct measurement.
- Record the R1 Blue Spot caliper piston configuration from authoritative
  evidence or documented measurement.
- Perform a documented hydraulic-ratio assessment before road use.
- Inspect the used master cylinder, lever, pivot, seals, reservoir, cap,
  diaphragm, clamp, threads, and brake-light-switch arrangement.
- Verify handlebar fit, reservoir orientation, lever travel, lever reach,
  switchgear clearance, throttle clearance, hose routing, banjo orientation,
  and full-steering-lock clearance.
- Define the required brake hose and banjo configuration.
- Perform a static pressure-hold and leak test after installation.
- Verify that the wheel releases correctly and that no residual brake pressure
  remains.
- Define acceptance criteria before controlled dynamic testing.
- Require technical review and prerequisite safety validation before any road
  test.
- Record successful installation, lever movement, or initial brake pressure as
  preliminary observations only; none constitutes validation.
