## Decision recommendation

**Recommendation: Bench evaluate**

**Rationale:** The pair is purchased and available, but physical identity,
condition, service-part applicability, fitment, hydraulic compatibility,
braking suitability, and final acceptance remain Unverified.

Do not use `Accept` while material braking and compatibility questions remain
unresolved.

## Required validation

- Photograph and record all physical markings before inferring application.
- Verify the reported part numbers and exact donor application using retained
  authoritative Yamaha evidence.
- Inspect and document external and internal condition, including pistons,
  seals, bores, threads, bleed screws, and sealing faces.
- Identify both repair kits and the EBC pads exactly before use; record kit
  contents and pad specifications only from retained evidence.
- Establish mounting, disc, pad-sweep, wheel, fork, hose, banjo, bleed, steering,
  and suspension clearances.
- Establish caliper piston configuration and complete a documented hydraulic
  assessment with COMP-0001.
- Define service and installation procedures and acceptance criteria.
- Perform documented static pressure-hold, leak, release, and residual-pressure
  tests before controlled dynamic testing.
- Complete technical review and prerequisite safety validation before road use.
