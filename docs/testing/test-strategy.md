# Test Strategy

**Purpose:** Define how testing and technical-review evidence will be recorded
for project work.

**Document status: Draft**

## Evidence

Link each completed test to its requirement, method, result, and any relevant
research or decision record.

## Safety review

Testing or claims involving braking, throttle control, fuel, ignition, or
electrical protection require technical review before being treated as
validated.
