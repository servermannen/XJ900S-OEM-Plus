# System Requirements

**Purpose:** Capture and maintain the project requirements that guide later
engineering work.

**Document status: Draft**

## Scope

Record functional, safety, serviceability, and integration requirements here
once they have been defined and reviewed.

## Requirement records

Each requirement should identify its status, rationale, source, and acceptance
method. Do not record assumptions as confirmed requirements.

## Safety-critical requirements

Requirements concerning braking, throttle control, fuel, ignition, or
electrical protection require technical review before acceptance.
